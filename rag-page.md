# What is RAG?

RAG stands for **Retrieval-Augmented Generation**.

The name sounds technical, but the basic idea is simple.

A normal language model knows general information from its training. It does not automatically know what is inside your private contracts, internal procedures, manuals, project files or business systems.

RAG connects AI to your own company knowledge.

When someone asks a question, the system first finds the relevant information from your approved sources.

It then gives that information to the AI model, which uses it to prepare the answer.

For document-based questions, the answer can also show where the information came from.

**Your data → relevant information → AI answer → source**

This makes information buried across documents and systems accessible through natural language.

# What happens when someone asks a question?

Imagine an employee asks:

**“Which customers have unpaid invoices and a contract renewal coming up in the next 60 days?”**

A simple document search is not enough.

The system may need to:

## 1. Understand the request

It identifies that the question requires both contract information and current invoice data.

## 2. Retrieve the relevant documents

It finds the customer contracts and extracts the relevant renewal information.

## 3. Use the right tools

It queries the accounting system, ERP or database for current unpaid invoices.

## 4. Combine the results

It matches the customers, checks the dates and prepares the result.

## 5. Return one answer

The user receives a clear response instead of manually checking several systems.

Where the answer is based on documents, the system can include the corresponding sources.
